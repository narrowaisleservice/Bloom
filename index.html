Reminders
Bloom can't reliably send push notifications as a home-screen web app, so this downloads a calendar file with your predicted period, PMS window and ovulation as events with a built-in alert — open it to add them to the phone's Calendar app. Re-download after logging a new period so the dates stay current.
Backup & restore
Everything's saved to your Google Sheet, so it's already safe across devices. Export gives you a local copy too; import replaces your sheet's data with a backup file.
Share with a partner
Copies a short, plain-text status update — today's phase, fertile window and next-period estimate — with no symptom, mood or intimacy details, ready to paste into a message.
For your doctor
Opens a clean, printable page with your cycle history and averages — use your phone's Print option and choose "Save to Files" or "Save as PDF" to bring to an appointment.
Reset all data?
This clears every logged period, symptom and note on this device. It can't be undone.
